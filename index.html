About

I am an experimental particle physicist and Assistant Professor at the University of Oklahoma. I am a member of the ATLAS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) where I work on searches for long-lived particles and other unconventional signs of new physics, charged-particle track reconstruction, and machine learning.

Before coming to Oklahoma, I was a postdoctoral fellow at Simon Fraser University. I received my PhD from the University of Massachusetts Amherst in 2021.
